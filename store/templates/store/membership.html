FAQ

Common Questions

When does my membership start?

Your membership activates immediately after payment. You'll receive a confirmation email and your discount will apply to your next order.

Can I switch plans?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Your new plan takes effect immediately and the price difference is prorated.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime from this page. Your membership remains active until the end of your current billing period — you won't be charged again.
